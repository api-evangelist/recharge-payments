Black Friday subscribers can be among a subscription brand's best customers — if the offer is built correctly. The key is using BFCM to sell quarterly and annual plans rather than discounting monthly prices. Annual subscribers retain nearly 6x better at month 13, and deeper discounts don't improve outcomes.
